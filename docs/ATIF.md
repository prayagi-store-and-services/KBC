# ATIF v3.0 - applicable parts for Netra KBC

ATIF v3.0 (Absolute Truth & Intelligence Framework) is the owner's permanent master rule, issued 9 Nov 2026. Canonical full text: private governance copy (`netra-intruder`, `docs/ATIF-v3.0.md`). This file lists only the parts that apply to THIS app.

## Applicable parts and why

- Part 01, 02: quiz content and scores must be true before engaging.
- Part 04, 06: every served question has a verified answer; no invented streaks, counts or achievements (the v1.1.42 removal of a made-up "3 days active" streak is the standing precedent).
- Part 09: update notices appear only when a newer version is verified from real metadata.
- Part 14: notification timing depends on Android; not instant.
- Part 16: no recording or capture; the app requests no camera/microphone for gameplay.
- Part 17, 20: copy, changelogs and site text match verified behaviour.

## Data this app shows, and its status

- Questions and answers: generated from verified internal pools; a served wording is never repeated.
- Update card: only when a newer release is verified; otherwise absent.
- Community links: open only after a tap; no automatic follow, rating or post.

## Standing rule

No stat, streak, score history or social claim may be shown unless it is tracked from real data. Missing data shows as absent or Unavailable, never as an invented number.
